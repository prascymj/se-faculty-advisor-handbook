Four core responsibilities

What you own as programme faculty

Each lists your duties, the granular evidence to keep, the AUN-QA criteria it supports, and the tools to use.

Responsibility 01

Academic Advisor

Guiding, monitoring & supporting your advisees

What you do

  • Meet each advisee at least once per semester (more for at-risk students).
  • Review study plan, GPA, credits & course selection each registration period.
  • Run early-warning: failing grades, absence, withdrawals, well-being signals.
  • Refer students needing help (financial, health, mental health) to the right unit.
  • Approve add/drop, leave & academic requests within deadlines.

Evidence to keep

  • Advising session record — date, topic, agreed action (de-identified).
  • Group/one-on-one advising attendance or screenshots (timestamped).
  • Early-warning entries — trigger + action + outcome.
  • Referral records to Counselling / Student Affairs (coded, no names).
  • Signed academic request forms — filed with the secretary.

Responsibility 02

Self-Development Plan

Your IDP, training & academic growth

What you do

  • Set an annual Individual Development Plan (IDP) aligned to your TOR and programme needs.
  • Plan training / conferences and request support through the School.
  • After training, record the impact — what you brought back into a course or research.
  • Maintain your publication and academic-position (promotion) trajectory.

Evidence to keep

  • IDP / TOR document for the year (goals + review).
  • Training certificates + post-training impact log → linked TQF3 change.
  • Publication record (journal / conference) with year & indexing.
  • Promotion-application evidence (when applicable).
  • Internal knowledge-sharing you led (Lab KM, workshop) — agenda + record.

Responsibility 03

Teaching Assessment & PLO / CLO Attainment

Showing how learning is measured & achieved

What you do

  • Map each course: CLO → PLO, and each CLO to a concrete assessment.
  • Publish rubrics with sub-criteria to students before the work begins.
  • Time assessment properly — release midterm results before the drop deadline.
  • Report attainment: Exit Exam → PLO1, Senior Project → PLO4, plus PLO2/3/6 soft skills.
  • Run course verification (ทวนสอบ) at end of term.

Evidence to keep

  • TQF3 with CLO→PLO mapping and teaching/assessment methods.
  • Rubric v2 (sub-criteria + level descriptors) as distributed.
  • Assessment timeline showing midterm-before-drop dates.
  • Rubric scores per student per CLO → % attainment table.
  • Exit Exam item map (→PLO1), SP rubric (→PLO4) with thresholds.
  • TQF5 + course verification; LMS grade-release & feedback records.

Student advising scenarios

Case-by-case playbook

Open a case for the steps, the form to use, and the evidence to capture.

01Add / Drop a courseC4 · operations
1
Check prerequisites, credit load, and timetable conflicts against the study plan.
2
Confirm the action is within the official add/drop window (see academic calendar).
3
Approve in REG; keep the signed / e-approved request.
KeepApproved add/drop request + reason if unusual (feeds ME-elective demand data for C2).
02Course withdrawal (W)C4 · assessment timing
1
Ensure the student has seen their midterm result before deciding — advise on realistic pass likelihood.
2
Discuss impact on progression and graduation timeline.
3
Approve within the W deadline; log the reason.
KeepWithdrawal reason (bottleneck course? workload?) → feeds late-graduation root-cause analysis (C8).
03Leave of absenceC6 · student support
1
Understand the reason (health, financial, personal) and expected return term.
2
Explain fee / registration rules for maintaining student status.
3
Approve the leave form; set a return check-in reminder.
KeepLeave record + follow-up plan. A leave that becomes non-return is an early dropout signal.
04Academic probation / dismissal riskC6 + C8 · early warning
1
Identify via GPA threshold / failed-credit count on the REG dashboard.
2
Hold a support meeting: study-plan adjustment, tutoring, workload reduction.
3
Log the intervention and monitor next-term outcome.
KeepEarly-warning entry (trigger → action → outcome). Core C6 evidence and prevents dropout.
05Resignation / dropout — with qualitative captureC8 · root cause
1
Student submits the withdrawal / resignation form (existing paper flow: student → secretary → advisor signs → dean signs).
2
At your signing moment, hold a short exit conversation (~10 min) and record: warning signals seen, the main factor in your view, help already given, whether it was preventable.
3
Invite the student to complete their own short exit form (their words + what could have helped + next plan) on their phone — consent-based.
4
Secretary adds objective facts (GPA, credits, admission channel) and files into the de-identified Dropout Register.
5
Silent dropout (no form, just stopped enrolling): advisor completes the record from known information and attempts contact.
PDPAKeep identity separate from the analysis register (use student code); consent is optional and does not affect the resignation.
CodingTag primary + up to 2 secondary causes (Academic / Fit / Financial / Health / Mental / Family / Transfer / Career / Other) so cases aggregate into a semester root-cause report.
06Mental-health concernC6 · well-being
1
Listen without judging; do not attempt to diagnose or counsel beyond your role.
2
Refer to the University Counselling / Wellness Center promptly; share the contact with the student.
3
Agree on academic accommodations if needed (deadlines, load) and a discreet follow-up.
PDPA / duty of careHealth data is sensitive — record only what is necessary, store securely, never on this page. Referral matters more than data.
07Grade appeal / grievanceC4 · fairness
1
Point the student to the rubric and grade-release evidence; explain the basis.
2
If unresolved, route to the formal appeal channel per University regulation.
3
Keep the rubric, scores, and correspondence as the transparency record.
KeepRubric + score sheet + resolution note (supports C4 fairness & transparency).
08Internship / co-op issueC1 + C8 · workplace
1
Coordinate with the site supervisor; clarify expectations and PLO-linked assessment.
2
Conduct the site visit; collect the employer evaluation and student reflection.
3
Escalate placement problems to the internship coordinator early.
KeepSite-visit report + employer evaluation → PLO feedback (C1) and employability data (C8).
09Change of major / transferC2 + C8 · retention
1
Understand the motivation (fit, interest, opportunity elsewhere).
2
Advise on credit transfer and process; complete the required forms.
3
Record as a distinct outcome — a transfer is not a failure-type dropout.
KeepTransfer reason in the register — separates "left for a better fit" from "left due to difficulty" in C8 analysis.
