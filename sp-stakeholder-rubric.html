Seminar → Senior Project — stakeholder score sheet

For industry partners, topic owners and external stakeholders to assess a student team against the programme's six Programme Learning Outcomes (PLOs, curriculum 2564). Usable at each stage — Seminar, Senior Project 1, and Senior Project 2 — so growth is comparable across the year. B.Eng. Software Engineering, School of Applied Digital Technology, MFU.

How to use · where your score goes Score each PLO on a 1–5 scale (N/A if you cannot judge it at this stage) — the totals update live. When done, choose one: Submit to coordinator (sends the result to the programme's secure Google Sheet), or Copy summary / Print / PDF to return it by hand or email. Your draft stays on this device until you submit or reset; nothing else is uploaded. Handle data under PDPA — refer to the team/topic, not students' personal data. The coordinator files each result in the SP Stakeholder Assessment sheet and as evidence under AUN-QA C4 / C8.

1 · What you are assessing

Senior Project 2 Assess the delivered product: a working, quality-assured system and its evaluation — score what the team actually built and the value it creates.

2 · Score each PLO (1 = well below · 3 = meets expectation · 5 = excellent / industry-ready)

PLO 1

Professionalism & responsibility

Acts with honesty and integrity, respects privacy and confidentiality, and takes responsibility with regard for users and the public interest.

1 · Below — careless with commitments, data or credit; needs prompting to act responsibly.
3 · Meets — reliable, honest, respects your data & deadlines.
5 · Excellent — proactively raises ethical/privacy issues; fully accountable and trustworthy.
PLO 2

Technology & business insight

employer priority

Connects key software technologies, methods and emerging/disruptive tech to the problem — and to its business or entrepreneurial value in your context.

1 · Below — picks tools without rationale; little grasp of the business need.
3 · Meets — sound technology choices linked to the requirement.
5 · Excellent — weighs trade-offs & emerging tech against clear business value.
PLO 3

Solution design & architecture

employer priority

Designs software architecture or an information-system structure that fits the problem, integrating appropriate technologies and related disciplines.

1 · Below — design unclear or unworkable for real use.
3 · Meets — coherent design that would work in practice.
5 · Excellent — robust, scalable design justified against alternatives.
PLO 4

Teamwork & collaboration

Shows effective leadership and followership and collaborates well with others, including across cultures and with your organisation.

1 · Below — uneven contribution; poor coordination with you.
3 · Meets — organised team; responsive and easy to work with.
5 · Excellent — strong shared ownership; leads and supports as needed.
PLO 5

Communication (incl. English) & tools

Presents and documents the work clearly — including in English — analyses data soundly, and uses appropriate development tools.

1 · Below — hard to follow; weak presentation or English.
3 · Meets — clear presentation & docs; adequate English.
5 · Excellent — confident, professional communication in English.
PLO 6

Working product & real-world value

employer priority

Delivers software that works and keeps pace with current technology, and could realistically create commercial or social value in your setting.

1 · Below — incomplete or not usable in practice.
3 · Meets — working system that addresses the need.
5 · Excellent — deployable; you would adopt or pilot it.
